Community-built plugins for Heretic.

Scorers, modifiers, configs, models, and tools made by the Heretic community. Each entry has a source, a guide, and a setup prompt.

  1. 01Krea2DriftMeasures how far an ablation shifts the text conditioning that Krea 2 feeds to its image model, without generating an image./scorer
  2. 02Krea2GoodMinimizes the p90 drift of good image prompts so an ablation leaves benign Krea 2 conditioning stable./scorer
  3. 03Krea2BadMaximizes the p90 drift of bad image prompts so abliteration visibly changes how the model handles them./scorer
  4. 04GibberishRateFlags trials whose responses turn into gibberish by counting distinct Unicode scripts./scorer
  5. 05Calibrated PIQAA Heretic fork with a benchmark scorer tuned to PIQA questions that flip between right and wrong./scorer
  6. 06LLM Judge scorerUses a separate language model to decide whether a response actually complies, instead of matching refusal keywords./scorer
  7. 07Refusal-direction scorer/taggerDetects refusals by comparing initial refusal directions with response hidden states instead of keyword lists./scorer
  8. 08Chinese censorship config forkA Heretic fork with refusal markers and prompts aimed at region-specific Chinese censorship rather than general refusals./config
  9. 09vantablack refusal classifierA refusal classification model for the BERTClassifier scorer, with separate full and partial refusal labels./model
  10. 10Refusal Classifier V3A family of refusal classifiers for BERTClassifier, published in small and large variants./model
  11. 11MPOA abliterationMagnitude-preserving orthogonal ablation, available through Heretic's directional abliteration modifier./modifier
  12. 12SOMA modifierA planned multi-directional modifier intended to sit alongside ARA as a shipped plugin./modifier
  13. 13Emotion steering configAn official non-default config that steers emotions by minimizing negative and maximizing positive labels./config
